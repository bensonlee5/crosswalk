Dalloway

June’s repaired bell could become an excellent way for grateful diners to ask for more.

The object

Dalloway’s Two-Note is a battered chrome bicycle bell clipped to the diner counter, its name stamped beside a worn thumb paddle. It rings a bright ding on the press and a lower dong when released. The return spring is ordinary and repairable; the recognizability comes from the two notes and the slightly ridiculous place where the bell has ended up.

In the proposed story, June owns the bell and restores its spring before a farewell to her station-announcing work. Esperanza’s “JUNE’S LAST ORDERS” tag and Kit’s empty-counter drawing would make the wrong workplace sound as though it were losing her. Meanwhile, appreciative customers could use her successful repair to summon still more service.

Brand & the object

Manufacturer of the proposed Two-Note bicycle bell.

A history of use

There is no established Dalloway episode. The proposal builds on June’s existing combination of cooking, bicycle repair and station announcements. Moving the bell by agreement to the kitchen pass, to announce finished plates, would change its demand on her. June would remain a cook; neither the bell nor somebody else’s farewell decides which work she leaves.

A proposed story use

Proposed storyline only: June repairs the bell before leaving station announcing, a farewell is mistaken for departure from the diner, and its eventual placement is decided with her. June’s announced decision and these events are not established biography.

This proposal is separate from the current game’s playable actions.
