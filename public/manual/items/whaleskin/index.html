The object

Whaleskin is a silver-grey waterproof fitted van cover with two conspicuously floppy wing-mirror sleeves and a bright stitched name label. Spread on the ground, it still looks unmistakably meant for a vehicle. Its advertised universal fit supplies the joke: on Lata’s van, the back would remain uncovered despite the price.

In the proposed story, Lata buys it and later gives the misfit to Tavi for sheltering produce-staging equipment beside a platform. That is a transfer of ownership, rather than an unexplained chain of borrowing. The loose hem would need proper assessment in its new setting. A recognizable sleeve could identify the material; only Mara’s professional inspection would establish what it had done.

Brand & the object

A history of use

This object has no established event history. It was devised from Lata’s delivery work, Tavi’s harbor growing and Mara’s maintenance expertise. Its proposed comic consequence is that the pinned waterproof edge obstructs runoff. A later photograph of the removed cover could make Lata look more heroic than the full sequence warrants.

A proposed story use

Proposed storyline only: Lata buys the badly fitting cover, gives it to Tavi, and Mara later establishes that its reused edge blocked runoff. These events have not happened in the game or the established biographies.

This proposal is separate from the current game’s playable actions.
