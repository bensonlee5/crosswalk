The object

The Bracken Redbank is a large dark-red bass drum with chrome lugs and a transparent front head. Nell’s own blue-check duvet is plainly visible inside, where bedding can legitimately damp the sound. A small maker’s mark stays secondary to that unmistakable circle of blue checks. The instrument is ordinary; its contents make an understatement difficult to sustain.

In the proposed story, Nell owns both drum and duvet. Ruth has allowed storage, then rents the room to Jonah. Calling the remaining obstruction “one drum” would minimize how much room and fallback lodging Nell still expects. Ruth’s hospitality and Jonah’s rent create different claims that need an actual conversation.

Brand & the object

Manufacturer of the proposed Redbank bass drum; Nell owns the drum and duvet in that proposal.

A history of use

Nell’s traveling work and amateur drumming, Ruth’s home, and Jonah’s wish for a room of their own provide the existing character foundations. The drum, storage arrangement and tenancy are new proposals. Moving it to rehearsal, travel or paid storage would change cost and access, but no destination automatically settles Nell’s relationships or requires her to choose a permanent home.

A proposed story use

Proposed storyline only: Nell’s drum and bedding remain in Ruth’s room after Jonah rents it, and the adults negotiate where the belongings will go. No such tenancy, storage agreement or resolution is established yet.

This proposal is separate from the current game’s playable actions.
