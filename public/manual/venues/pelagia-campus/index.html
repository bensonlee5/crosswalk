Tideworks College

Tideworks College is the harbor’s learning address, where everyday ecology and practical construction provide a setting for study. You can arrive with a question rather than a finished explanation.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Its character grows from the relationship between maintained waterways and the surrounding growing country. Classrooms sit close enough to working life for a discussion to have concrete examples, yet learning has its own pace. The college’s story is about giving people room to acquire skills, not granting an unearned engineering title after a single visit.

Architecture & everyday space

Low teaching pavilions stand on a raised base, linked by a shaded passage. One room favors shared project tables; another is small enough for a tutorial. Broad roofs and glazed upper panels give useful daylight while leaving wall space for diagrams and student work.

Weather & the setting

Monsoon rain can be loud on a roof, so interior study areas sit behind the more open circulation edge. Shutters and high vents let the rooms respond to humid heat and a sudden squall.

Its place in community life

A month of peer study has a different social texture from an intensive course. Learners bring experience from households, boats or gardens without needing to speak for an entire community. Its scale leaves room to ask a question without needing to make an occasion of it.
