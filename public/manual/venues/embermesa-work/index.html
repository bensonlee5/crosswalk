Roster House

Roster House is a practical working stop on the rural loop. The building brings desks and bounded paid tasks together while the workshops, gardens and homes around it remain spread out.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The settlement’s history joins an old railway town to modern repair and solar-roof work. These rooms keep that combination visible: a modest former service building can hold contemporary administration without surrendering its weathered proportions. June’s diner-and-repair roster gives regional work here a particularly local purpose.

Architecture & everyday space

A long, low timber shed has a deep front porch and a simpler service side. The interior divides into desk space, a shared table and storage rather than a row of identical offices. High openings admit light above the working surfaces, with heavier shade on the sun-facing edge.

Weather & the setting

The distance between buildings makes exposed arrivals part of a hot day. The shaded threshold provides a pause, and ventilated upper space helps the interior lose heat as the air cools later.

Its place in community life

June’s regional shift involves safe desk tasks and coordination; trained repairers handle brakes and wiring. The workrooms support a visitor’s paid contribution without recruiting them into every town responsibility. The same door also leads to the player’s continuing career work.
