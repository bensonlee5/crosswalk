Brickhouse Library

Brickhouse Library connects reading with the small practical exchanges of the quay. Rowan’s returns trolley and bicycle basket make books and growing things familiar neighbors here.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Rowan’s seed swap began beside the library returns trolley. The gardening club grew partly from wanting company, and knowledge accumulated through trying things together. That modest origin matters more than an impressive opening ceremony: lending and learning can start at the scale somebody can maintain. Later tool-loan programs belong to the evolving game rather than being assumed from the first visit.

Architecture & everyday space

A substantial brick room holds shelves against sheltered walls and reading tables near taller windows. The returns area has space for a small display without blocking the reading room. Low lamps and visible handwritten labels make the interior feel used rather than staged.

Weather & the setting

Weatherproof returns storage, an inner coat space and paper kept back from draughty windows answer the persistent damp. On a cold afternoon, the interior offers a different kind of quiet from the exposed river.

Its place in community life

Rowan is a librarian with interests of their own, as well as a volunteer gardener. Their curiosity connects a written explanation with something a neighbor can try. The room also suits Priya’s careful weather observations, with space for someone to read quietly instead of producing another report.
