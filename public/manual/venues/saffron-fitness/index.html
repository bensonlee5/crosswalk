Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The studio took form in a courtyard building whose broad rooms could accommodate movement more readily than another set of small shops. Changes to the interior emphasized a clear floor and places to put belongings, leaving the older courtyard arrangement recognizable outside.

Architecture & everyday space

A restrained frontage opens into a bright principal room, with smaller supporting spaces kept to one side. High windows admit light while giving the main floor some privacy. The adjacent court creates a visual breathing space between the studio and the next cluster of homes.

Weather & the setting

Warm, dry summers make shade and moving air central to the building’s comfort. Winter rain changes the approach more than the interior, where the enclosed floor offers a different setting from wet park paths.

Its place in community life

The studio shows the town keeping ordinary habits between its more conspicuous evenings. Its neighborhood scale suits repeat visits rather than spectacle, and different working hours bring changing company. The courtyard gives arrivals a moment to settle before entering the main room.
