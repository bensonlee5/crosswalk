The Goods Exchange

The Goods Exchange brings provisions into the compact network around Glasssteppe’s railway. Its scale suits people carrying a modest purchase uphill, combining the railway’s wider reach with the immediate requirements of a neighborhood supper.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The market developed where arriving goods and residents’ daily routes already met. Covered selling space allowed trade to continue through weather that made an exposed square less dependable. Subsequent alterations retained the market’s direct relationship with circulation rather than turning it into an isolated hall.

Architecture & everyday space

Stalls fit within a strong stone envelope, with sheltered passages and broad openings at the ends. Crates, counters and hanging signs introduce smaller geometries inside the larger structural bays. Copper-toned details link the building visually to the roofs above the surrounding terraces.

Weather & the setting

Cold weather makes the difference between covered trading space and the outdoor approach immediately apparent. Snowmelt follows deliveries and shoppers inside during thaws. Summer brings longer daylight to the entrances without removing the market’s useful shelter.

Its place in community life

Visit for the everyday relationship between railway supply and a household supper. Sellers and shoppers discuss availability, taste and what can be carried uphill. The market’s compactness makes those practical choices visible, from the counter to the bag heading toward the next terrace.
