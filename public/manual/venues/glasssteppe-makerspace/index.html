The Copper Bench

The Copper Bench makes Glasssteppe’s repair culture tangible in benches, recovered parts and drawings left where another pair of hands can understand them. The railway supplies part of its material history, while useful objects eventually return to many different kinds of homes and work.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Sora began with salvaged station fittings and tools borrowed one at a time. Wear on recovered pieces offered evidence to examine before choosing their next use. Clear diagrams developed alongside the practical kit, never merely as decoration.

Architecture & everyday space

A dense stone workshop encloses substantial tables and orderly stretches of storage. Copper and other recovered metals bring varied surfaces to the subdued room. High light reveals edges and fastenings, while drawings and visibly worked labels make the space understandable at close range.

Weather & the setting

Winter draws work inside, away from platform winds and snow. Cold materials still carry the season indoors. Summer light lasts longer over the benches, with open windows admitting railway sound.

Its place in community life

Sora’s repair work anchors this venue’s character. Their exchange with Miro, a repaired projection lamp for hand-painted drawer labels, connects practical care with performance. Visit for the material detail: worn edges, visible brushwork and explanations that remain useful after their maker leaves the bench.
