Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The clockhouse is one of the quay’s established landmarks. The workrooms grew around its dependable timekeeping: a former tally room gains shared desks, while a service bay keeps its broad doors. The older purpose remains legible without assigning the player a particular port trade or changing their chosen career.

Architecture & everyday space

A solid brick front faces the compact street grid. Tall workshop windows light long surfaces; a gabled service wing sits beside smaller office rooms. The contrast between an open bench and a quiet desk suits several kinds of work under the same local name.

Weather & the setting

Covered approaches limit exposure during a rainy change of shift. Matting, coat hooks and protected paper storage keep river damp from becoming the organizing principle of the whole room.

Its place in community life

Tess’s regional lunch-round work links the café, crew orders and dispatch timetable here. A useful shift has agreed limits and a finish; nobody’s ability to organize a busy room makes them permanently available.
