Tidal Library

Tidal Library is worth visiting for the harbor’s quieter voices. Its shelves make space for the ordinary lives behind the famous domes, alongside the resources for reading, study and shared discussion.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Esme gathered neighbors’ handwritten stories because lunches and friendships belong in a town’s record as surely as storms do. This concern gives the library a specific historical purpose without turning every conversation into material to collect. A later listening-alcove program develops in the game; it should be discovered as a change rather than presumed on arrival.

Architecture & everyday space

The reading rooms occupy a raised, enclosed building with pale surfaces and carefully placed windows. Shelves sit within the dry center; reading tables take the softer perimeter light. A sheltered entrance provides a pause before wet clothing and loose papers reach the collection.

Weather & the setting

Humidity and monsoon rain demand protected storage, ventilation and care over where paper is left. The surrounding water can be beautiful without being a harmless backdrop to an archive.

Its place in community life

Esme’s approach leaves the teller in charge of what is shared. Readers can spend time with a book or join a discussion without donating a life story. The paid digitization activity has its own skill condition; affection for local history does not erase the work involved.
