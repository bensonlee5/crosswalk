Highwater Promenade

Highwater Promenade is Pelagia’s public place to slow down above the water. It combines the openness of a harbor walk with planted edges and seats that let a view become part of an ordinary afternoon.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The promenade belongs to the town’s practical habit of lifting everyday routes above working basins. Its park character comes from retaining room to pause along those routes rather than filling every dry surface with another building. Tavi’s interest in plants beside salt water gives the planted margins a connection to local growing knowledge.

Architecture & everyday space

A broad raised path bends through seating pockets and planted sections. Railings define the water-facing edge, while low roofs provide intermittent shade rather than enclosing the whole park. The arrangement keeps the harbor visible and gives resting places an identity beyond a transit corridor.

Weather & the setting

Heat, reflected glare and sudden heavy rain all change the experience. Sheltered pauses are valuable, but an outdoor promenade remains exposed compared with a room; weather belongs in the decision about how long to stay.

Its place in community life

Tavi is associated with both the park and the garden, which remain distinct destinations. Sefu also visits on a changing schedule. A short walk or a quiet reading session needs no gardening achievement and no contribution to harbor upkeep.
