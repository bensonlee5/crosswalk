Stillwater Studio

Stillwater Studio is the sheltered counterpart to Pelagia’s open walks. Come for a class, a structured session or recovery when humid weather makes the outdoor route less appealing.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The studio’s history follows a small harbor’s need to reuse limited raised space carefully. A broad room accommodates changing kinds of movement rather than demanding a separate building for each one. Its purpose is ordinary wellbeing, independent of the more conspicuous engineering that supports the surrounding town.

Architecture & everyday space

A lightweight pavilion sits above the damp ground line, with a solid central floor and screened edges. A compact front desk separates arriving visitors from the active room. Equipment storage keeps the main surface clear, while a quieter recovery area has a more enclosed character.

Weather & the setting

Shade and cross-ventilation temper warm, wet air. Screens reduce glare from the water, and closable panels keep wind-driven rain off the interior when a shower turns into a squall.

Its place in community life

The view can be restful, and a class gives the day a different structure from an errand or a walk. Company is welcome without turning every session into a group undertaking. This is another way to enjoy the harbor, alongside its more visible culture of work, study and upkeep.
