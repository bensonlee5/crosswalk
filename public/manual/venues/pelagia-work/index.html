Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Mara’s flood-gate work gives the place a clear human connection. Pumps and gates need continuing attention, and the people planning that care need somewhere to compare notes and explain useful information. The workrooms’ history is built around making essential, sometimes unnoticed work visible without treating every visitor as an engineer.

Architecture & everyday space

A raised service building has a broad roof and open-sided circulation around enclosed work areas. Tall tables keep paper above the damp floor edge; a public-facing desk is separate from equipment and technical storage. Glazed panels catch the watery light without requiring every room to be a decorative dome.

Weather & the setting

Deep eaves handle heavy rain while cross-ventilation tempers humid heat. Sheltered approaches connect the desks to the boardwalk network; wind-driven water still makes proper storage part of daily work.

Its place in community life

Mara’s regional job concerns a public tide-safe crossing board, with technical inspection left to trained engineers. The workrooms also support the player’s existing career. Being useful here need not mean promising to settle or taking responsibility for machinery.
