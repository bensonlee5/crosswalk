Cottonwood Park

Cottonwood Park makes a small patch of shade feel significant against the open mesa. It is a place to sit, read or take a measured walk without pretending the surrounding desert is a broad green lawn.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Its history is one of choosing where a settlement can care for trees and leave ground open. The park’s value grows from restraint: a few established shade places and worn routes can serve neighbors better than an ambitious landscape nobody has time or water to maintain. It gives outdoor pleasure a public place in a craft-centered town.

Architecture & everyday space

Cottonwoods gather over selected sitting areas, with sparse planting and unpaved routes between them. The boundaries remain visually open to the wider land. A small sheltered pause sits within the landscape rather than trying to wall off the horizon.

Weather & the setting

Shade is uneven and the dry heat is real; cooler parts of a day have a different appeal. Paths can be dusty, and seasonal weather changes the experience. A described shelter does not certify every approach as physically accessible.

Its place in community life

Kit appears here on their recurring schedule, with art and life beyond the repair culture around them. Reading under a tree can be a complete outing. The park offers rest independently of any contribution to the town garden.
