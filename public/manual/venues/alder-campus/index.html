Bellweather College

Bellweather College is a small urban learning address, fitted into the quay rather than placed beyond it. A class can sit between paid work, an errand and the trip home.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Its history is one of adaptation: practical teaching takes over rooms with good daylight, shared tables replace isolated desks, and old workshop proportions remain useful for new subjects. The college is not given a grand founding legend. Its importance comes from letting adults return to learning without pretending they have left the rest of life behind.

Architecture & everyday space

A broad brick teaching block has the gabled silhouette of neighboring work buildings. Higher windows hold the light over project tables, while smaller side rooms allow a tutorial or a quiet explanation. The doorway faces the public street rather than an enclosed ceremonial campus.

Weather & the setting

Deep sills and a covered threshold provide a place to shake off drizzle before opening a notebook. Winter afternoons call for warm interior light; summer study is shaped by Alder’s generally mild air.

Its place in community life

Peer study shares both materials and uncertainty. Tutors bring knowledge earned elsewhere, and learners can ask basic questions without a public performance.
