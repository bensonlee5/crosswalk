Kiln & Current

Kiln & Current carries the material history of Saffron Reach’s canal edges indoors. Tile, repaired fittings and careful handwork give it a relationship with the water even when the work on a particular bench belongs to a much smaller object.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The makerspace developed around maintaining surfaces and useful things that weather beside the canals. Its ceramic name records that foundation, while the work widened with the neighborhood. Tomas’s boat knowledge and Sami’s tile restoration fit different parts of this lineage.

Architecture & everyday space

Sturdy tables sit beneath high, shaded openings, with storage arranged to keep small components distinct. Washable surfaces and a sheltered working court make the building less domestic than its neighboring courtyards. Muted clay colors are interrupted by glazed fragments and the occasional bright diagram.

Weather & the setting

Dry-season dust gathers at the open edges, while summer light becomes gentler deeper inside the workshop. Winter rain emphasizes the value of keeping materials under cover instead of beside an exposed canal wall.

Its place in community life

Tomas brings boat-repair knowledge; Sami brings tile restoration and playful tabletop forms. Their friendship includes a reused crate lid and a retiled shelf. The makerspace rewards attention to small materials, where the canal district’s working history becomes visible at hand scale.
