Gatehouse Landing

Gatehouse Landing is the useful front door between Pelagia and the wider world. The most valuable thing here may be a clear explanation of a crossing rather than a spectacular view of it.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Mara’s tide-mark explanations connect the station to the work that makes travel legible. The harbor’s engineering history is a history of people translating changing water into workable routines. Later, a tide-safe crossing board becomes a regional program; the exact opening belongs to the game’s passing months.

Architecture & everyday space

A raised waiting hall meets branching boardwalks, with ticket and information surfaces set back under a deep roof. A bicycle repair area occupies a separate working edge. The public desk is distinct from gates, pumps and other technical systems, preserving a useful boundary between information and inspection.

Weather & the setting

Heavy rain, warm humidity and wind over open water make sheltered waiting worthwhile. A covered hall does not remove exposure from the rest of a journey; the live travel preview is the guide to the current game’s weather effects.

Its place in community life

Mara and Sefu have verified associations with this transit place. Neri’s later story contact can happen here or through a remote call. A visit keeps the traveler’s existing home, while relocation is a separate apartment decision.
