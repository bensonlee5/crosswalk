Common Table Hall

Common Table Hall makes room for pantry work, practical workshops and gatherings that need more chairs than someone’s kitchen. It is a public room within the dense neighborhood, not a grand civic monument.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The quay’s history of shared suppers and small contributions gives this room its purpose. Eli’s useful herb harvest belonged to that wider pantry life; other residents bring time, skills or company. Furniture is repaired and rearranged as needs change, with an old table remaining useful long after the activity around it has changed.

Architecture & everyday space

A wide brick doorway leads to a rectangular hall with a clear central floor. Folded tables sit along one wall; a serving counter and dry store take the working end. Noticeboards and family corners occupy the edges rather than forcing every gathering into one arrangement.

Weather & the setting

A roofed loading recess keeps donation boxes out of rain. Washable entrance surfaces and high windows make the room practical in wet months without turning the building into an emergency claim.

Its place in community life

Celia sometimes brings painting and dominoes for her own enjoyment. Oren, Hugo and Priya also appear here on their schedules. Their presence makes a shared place richer without making them an unlimited volunteer workforce.
