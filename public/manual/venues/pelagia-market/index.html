First Ferry Market

First Ferry Market is where the boats and the growing hinterland meet a lunch appetite. Stop for adaptable food, conversation around a stall and a sense of what arrived safely today.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Lio’s first shared broth pot connects the market to early ferry workers. Tavi’s patient experiments connect it to growing beside salt water, with dependable vegetables as important as an interesting trial. Their later shared harvest table is an evolving regional program; the underlying market already has a life of its own.

Architecture & everyday space

Covered stalls stand on a raised deck with a generous central aisle. The roof breaks into separate bays so light and air reach the working surfaces. Food preparation sits back from the edge; the public frontage gives customers somewhere to wait without occupying a loading route.

Weather & the setting

Monsoon storms can delay supplies and drive rain sideways under an awning. Covered storage, flexible menus and shaded working space make more sense here than a promise of identical ingredients every day.

Its place in community life

Lio explains substitutions instead of treating them as an embarrassment. Tavi’s growing work gives the food conversations a connection to the country beyond the harbor. The visitor’s cooking lesson, market lunch and paid stall work are distinct ways to spend time here.
