Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

It began as a desk people could find without entering a large administrative building. Its rooms expanded around that simple need: somewhere to lay out a project, check an application, and speak without a queue listening. The modest name remains even after the desk needs more than one room.

Architecture & everyday space

A side-facing brick frontage distinguishes it from the larger workrooms. A glazed entrance gives onto a waiting alcove and several small interview spaces; a long shared surface can hold a portfolio without taking over a corridor. Notices are close to the door rather than hidden upstairs.

Weather & the setting

The entrance recess offers cover from river rain. A draught lobby and places for wet umbrellas keep papers and conversations away from the worst of the weather.

Its place in community life

The desk links personal ambition with a dense network of ordinary workplaces. A meetup can produce a useful introduction, but a conversation is not itself a job offer. Its history leaves room for people returning, changing direction or deciding to keep their present role.
