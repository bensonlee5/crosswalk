Willow Park

Willow Park is a pause in the close-built quay. Its trees and small public seats serve people with a spare hour as readily as those setting out for a longer walk.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Gardens occupy gaps and courtyards throughout Alder; this park gives that greenery a public address. Its history follows a pocket of ground kept open while the working streets grew around it. The planted edges, worn paths and repaired seats suggest repeated local use rather than a landscape made only to be admired from a distance.

Architecture & everyday space

A low boundary lets the park remain visible from the street. Willow branches soften the brick backdrop, and a loop of paths passes open sitting places and quieter corners. The layout offers a distinct outdoor room without implying that the painted scene is an exact surveyed plan.

Weather & the setting

Rain collects in leaf tips and darkens the paving before the streets dry. Draining paths and sheltered edges matter in the maritime climate; a bright break can change the whole character of an afternoon.

Its place in community life

Bram checks the usefulness of routes and resting places, while Priya enjoys noticing weather without producing another report. Their interests give the park texture, but visitors can also run, read or simply stop without undertaking a project.
