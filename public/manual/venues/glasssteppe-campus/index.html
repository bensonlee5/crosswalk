Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The college expanded through neighboring terrace buildings rather than one level campus. Rooms changed purpose as courses developed, but connections retained the hillside’s older logic. Learning here has a physical history of adaptation as well as an institutional one.

Architecture & everyday space

Stone facades and copper roof edges form a stepped outline, with covered connections where adjoining buildings meet. Deep windows light teaching rooms without opening them directly to the busiest passage. Small landings serve as pauses between enclosed spaces and changes in height.

Weather & the setting

Cold winters encourage people to gather just inside doorways rather than linger on exposed steps. Snow can remain along shaded exterior edges. The brief mild summer brings open windows and a stronger sense of the surrounding mountains.

Its place in community life

The college is worth noticing for the way learning occupies the same hillside as practical trades. Questions range from a particular diagram to subjects far beyond the railway. Between classes, small landings briefly become the city’s most concentrated patches of conversation.
