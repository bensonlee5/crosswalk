Casa del Patio

Casa del Patio is Saffron Reach’s flexible indoor gathering place, opening onto the kind of shared exterior space that gives the district its social shape. It can hold conversation without requiring an entire evening to revolve around a performance.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The room developed as a dependable counterpart to gatherings held outside. Chairs and tables no longer had to be borrowed from several homes whenever rain threatened. Its history lies in repeated rearrangement: an open floor one afternoon, a circle of seats on another.

Architecture & everyday space

A broad opening faces the courtyard, while solid side walls provide quieter edges and storage for furniture. The interior keeps its finishes simple. Light from the court reaches across the floor without competing with the people or objects gathered there.

Weather & the setting

In dry weather the threshold connects indoor conversation with the cooler evening air. Winter gatherings draw farther inside, with the court retaining the sound of rain after the room has warmed.

Its place in community life

Ada’s organizing connects the room to the district’s film culture, while Sami brings a different tabletop interest. It is worth noticing how a plain shared room accommodates such distinct uses: the furniture changes, but the neighborhood remains recognizable around it.
