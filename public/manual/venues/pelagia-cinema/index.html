The Lantern Dome

The Lantern Dome is a compact screen room that borrows Pelagia’s curved roofline for an everyday pleasure. It offers an enclosed pause after a day of bright water and open walkways.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Its history begins with the simple need for a dark, dry gathering room in a town famous for its exterior views. A small auditorium and ticket area make entertainment part of local life instead of something reserved for visitors. The building’s name does not make it the enormous central dome pictured in the regional painting.

Architecture & everyday space

A shallow curved roof covers a rectangular seating plan, with an enclosed projection end and a small sheltered foyer. Narrow bands of daylight belong to the entrance; curtains keep the screen room focused. Raised foundations continue the practical language of neighboring public buildings.

Weather & the setting

A generous entry canopy lets people gather during rain, while the enclosed auditorium separates the film from glare and humid wind. Seasonal showers still shape the approach along the open harbor walks.

Its place in community life

A matinee and a film discussion bring different audiences and moods. A visitor can join a conversation or simply leave with the story still in mind. The cinema’s shared darkness has its own appeal in a town so often approached through open views and bright water.
