Clocktower Arcade

Clocktower Arcade is an indoor place to play within the quay’s practical street network. A game table can occupy a whole evening without needing to justify itself as work, training or community service.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Its history borrows the neighborhood’s habit of keeping useful things in circulation: cabinets are repaired, chairs are replaced individually, and favorite games remain beside newer arrivals. The clocktower name gives it a local reference point, but the guide does not identify an actual tower room as an additional explorable floor.

Architecture & everyday space

A plain brick frontage opens into a long room with cabinets along the walls and larger shared tables farther back. Pockets of colored light sit against the warmer masonry. A staffed desk can see the entrance without making the social tables feel like a queue.

Weather & the setting

A recessed doorway and coat space keep the rainy street out of the playing area. On a mild evening, conversation can drift to the threshold; the main games remain an indoor shelter from coastal showers.

Its place in community life

Some visitors come alone to play a familiar favorite; others want a seat at a tabletop night. Hosting asks for people as well as a room. The arcade gives Alder’s community life a lighter corner, where sharing an evening can be enough without ending in a useful neighborhood project.
