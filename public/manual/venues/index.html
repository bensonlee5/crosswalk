Practical guide to visiting
  • The five towns contain 75 visitable places. Each has a local proper name, with its everyday function shown alongside it. Town portraits illustrate the regional atmosphere rather than a surveyed building plan.
  • Local-history, architecture and atmosphere passages are authored worldbuilding grounded in the established town and character canon. They do not add interactable rooms, opening-hour systems or tested physical-access certifications.
  • The “In the current game” sections describe new monthly shared-story games. Follow the current preview for money, time, paid work hours and what is available to your character. Costs, recovery, weather, learning and story circumstances can affect an activity. Older saved rooms keep their earlier rules.
  • Moving locally to a discovered, open place is free. The labeled Places list is an alternative to selecting a map marker, and the game also exposes it with the L key. This is an implemented navigation option, not a claim of tested browser, mobile-device or physical-building accessibility.
  • People links can mean an owner, worker, historical contributor or local association; the descriptions distinguish them. Current encounters vary as the month and personal story develop. Remote contacts remain remote, and someone’s hometown does not promise their presence at every local venue.
  • Exploring, ordinary activities and local introductions help you discover a town. Compact Alder and Glasssteppe favor nearby, sheltered service routes; Saffron, Pelagia and Ember Mesa have more open stretches where weather is worth considering. Follow your current map for discovered and open places.
  • Places develop over time, and local participation can help. Return to see what has changed without assuming a journey itself causes growth. An added program qualifier belongs to the same venue, and the current notice explains what you can join.
