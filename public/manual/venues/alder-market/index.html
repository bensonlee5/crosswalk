Ferry Bell Market

Ferry Bell Market gathers food, casual work and secondhand selling around Alder’s working-day appetite. Its name belongs to the flow of people and supplies through the quay, not to a single stallholder.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Covered stalls grew beside the routines of ferry crews: provisions before a crossing, a cooked meal between shifts, and produce brought in from the neighborhood’s small growing spaces. Tess’s advance-order counter is a later regional program with an actual game condition, so the base market’s history leaves its arrival in the future.

Architecture & everyday space

A brick-edged market hall breaks into narrow bays under one practical roof. Serving counters face a shared aisle; a side court can hold temporary tables when the weather allows. Goods sit above the floor, and glazed roof strips put useful light onto working surfaces.

Weather & the setting

Awnings overlap enough to keep the main stall fronts sheltered during showers. The court remains open to the sea air, so stall cloths, dry stores and a quick way to cover produce are everyday necessities.

Its place in community life

Hugo’s breadmaking gives him a practical connection to the market’s food culture, while Tess’s later lunch-round program links meals with ferry work. A visitor can learn to cook, enjoy a market lunch or take on paid stall work. These are different ways of spending time in the same busy room.
