Salt & Seed Garden

Salt & Seed Garden shows the growing country reaching into Pelagia’s waterside life. Its beds are a practical response to place, with reliable greens sharing attention with a small number of experiments.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Tavi tests ways to grow familiar vegetables near salt water. A failed experimental crop is part of that history, and so is the quieter success of dependable greens feeding people. The garden therefore rewards close looking: an ordinary healthy row can carry more useful knowledge than the most unusual plant.

Architecture & everyday space

Growing beds sit above the harbor edge, separated by working paths and small shaded pauses. Containers and protected planting areas let growers vary conditions at a manageable scale. A simple greenhouse or covered work area serves the plants rather than competing with the city’s domes.

Weather & the setting

Warmth can encourage growth, while heavy rain and saline exposure complicate it. Raised beds, drainage and attention to freshwater give the garden its form. The rural hinterland shares these ecological questions without being reduced to a decorative background.

Its place in community life

Tavi can offer company and knowledge without promising every trial will succeed. The later harvest connection with Lio has a particular game opening. A visitor can tend, learn, help with the harvest or rest; sitting quietly does not make them responsible for the whole plot.
