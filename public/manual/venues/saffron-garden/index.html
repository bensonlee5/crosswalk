Above the Arcade

Above the Arcade brings a more enclosed form of growing above Saffron Reach’s low buildings. From the planted roof, nearby courts and terracotta edges read as a patchwork rather than one continuous street frontage.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The garden began as a way to use a bare upper surface for something besides catching the sun. Containers and defined beds developed gradually, shaped by the roof’s exposure and the practical limits of carrying materials upstairs.

Architecture & everyday space

Low parapets frame planted sections, a clear circulation strip and modest resting places. The architecture stays subordinate to the growing space: pale surfaces, simple vessels and shelter positioned where it can make a difference. The ceramic water tower remains a fitting landmark in the wider view.

Weather & the setting

The roof receives more sun and wind than a shaded courtyard below. Dry summers demand careful water use, while winter rain changes the feel of open paving. Evening cool arrives with a greater sense of exposure.

Its place in community life

Visit for a closer view of growing and a wider view of the roofs. A changed leaf or shifting patch of shade matters here more than a grand display. Its small scale offers a different pause from the paths of Lemonshade Park.
