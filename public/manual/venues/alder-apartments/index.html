Rainwell Court

Rainwell Court puts private domestic life inside Alder’s compact working streets. A modest kitchen, a window plant and a room that can be reset matter as much here as the view of the river.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The building belongs to the quay’s tradition of adjoining brick homes rather than a separate residential estate. Successive small alterations have made room for different households: a divided landing, a repaired cupboard, a table carried carefully upstairs. Its history is the accumulation of ordinary tenancies, not the claim that every named neighbor lives behind the same door.

Architecture & everyday space

Rain-dark masonry and gabled roofs continue the street wall. Deep window reveals make sheltered ledges; a narrow shared entrance opens into a quieter interior, where coats and muddy shoes can be left before reaching the living space. Rooms feel compact without being described as identical.

Weather & the setting

A porch catches the first rain from arriving coats. Ventilated storage and places to dry cloth are practical responses to the wet maritime air; mild summer evenings can bring an open window.

Its place in community life

A potluck begins as an invitation into someone’s own space. The surrounding port is sociable, but a closed door also makes room for rest, music and a meal without an audience.
