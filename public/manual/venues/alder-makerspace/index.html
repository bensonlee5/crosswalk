Foundry Makerspace

Foundry Makerspace is the quay’s address for useful repair and work done by hand. Its benches belong to people making decisions about materials, credit and what a job can reasonably ask of them.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Jo learned by keeping well-used neighborhood things working. Labeled offcuts, useful tools and an inherited broken sign tie their story to repair rather than novelty for its own sake. An old foundry shell has been adapted to smaller, cleaner everyday work, leaving the heavy outline while changing what happens inside.

Architecture & everyday space

A tall gabled roof spans shared benches and a deeper service bay. Copper scraps, timber pieces and labeled storage give each working surface a purpose; a clear aisle separates visitors from active tasks. Broad windows bring light onto a joint or a written instruction.

Weather & the setting

Rain is kept off tools at the entrance and damp materials have somewhere to dry. The large brick enclosure is sheltered, but the coastal air still makes careful storage and ordinary maintenance necessary.

Its place in community life

Jo’s repair work and Oren’s miniature making need different scales of attention. Both make the workshop’s materials feel particular: a useful copper scrap for one task, a piece of tiny scenery for another. Clearing a bench for company should still leave its maker a say over the work and its finish.
