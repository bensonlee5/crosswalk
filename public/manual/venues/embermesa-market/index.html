Sundown Exchange

Sundown Exchange is a modest supply point for a town whose households are spread across open land. Food, a stall conversation and a useful secondhand find give the stop its everyday appeal.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The railway gives the market a connection beyond the mesa, while local gardens contribute on a smaller scale. Its history grows from goods being gathered where people already need to pass, then making room for cooks and temporary sellers. The later evening supper tables are a regional development rather than a permanent first-day festival.

Architecture & everyday space

A cluster of shallow timber stalls sits beneath a long shared awning. Open sides allow the market to remain part of the street; enclosed stores protect what cannot sit in the heat. Temporary tables have a clear place without requiring another large building.

Weather & the setting

Shade matters most through dry, bright hours. Cooler evenings make the same market edge sociable in a different way, while covered storage protects supplies from dust and occasional rain.

Its place in community life

June’s later supper program borrows tables and relies on a shared roster. The market’s ordinary food and work activities continue around that use. A customer can enjoy what is available without assuming a whole rural supply network depends on their purchase or help.
