Willowbank Allotments

Willowbank Allotments fits food-growing into the narrow spaces of an urban quay. Small plots, containers and a greenhouse make the scale intimate enough that a single useful harvest can be recognized.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

Eli began with borrowed containers and brought herbs to a supper assembled from small contributions. Rowan’s seed exchange and volunteer gardening provide another route into the allotments. These histories keep the place grounded in learning and repeated care, rather than describing a perfected garden that has never lost a seedling.

Architecture & everyday space

Beds and paths fill a gap between brick boundaries. The greenhouse makes a bright, enclosed working end, while water vessels and tool storage sit close to the growing area. A bench offers a place to pause that does not require standing among somebody else’s plants.

Weather & the setting

Good drainage is as important as water supply in Alder’s wet climate. Raised containers, clear paths and protection for tender plants let growers respond to chilly rain and brief bright spells without pretending every season is favorable.

Its place in community life

Eli’s pantry work and Rowan’s shared gardening rota intersect here. Shared suppers give the harvest a destination beyond the beds. Looking after the garden also means leaving room for its regulars to stop for the day.
