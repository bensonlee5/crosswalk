Farther Shore Careers

Pelagia’s Farther Shore Careers serves a small town with a wide working hinterland. Its appeal is practical: a dry place to put a portfolio, ask about a next role and turn an introduction into a considered choice.

Worldbuilding notes

The history, architecture and community description below expand this fictional place. Gameplay facts are listed separately.

A local history

The desk’s history follows the need for a common meeting point among scattered homes, growing areas and harbor services. A public counter becomes a small suite of rooms, but the purpose stays modest. It helps connect work and people without promising that every useful skill must lead to the same career.

Architecture & everyday space

A compact raised building branches from the public walk. The front counter shares light with a waiting bench; more private conversations take place behind an inner partition. A flat work surface is large enough for drawings without borrowing space from a busy passage.

Weather & the setting

A projecting roof keeps much of the seasonal rain away from arriving documents. Ventilated storage and a sheltered threshold suit the damp air, while adjustable shutters temper glare reflected off the water.

Its place in community life

Visitors can consider professional plans while keeping a life elsewhere. Engineering and ecology supply much of Pelagia’s public context, but an individual’s next step can draw on a different interest. A good introduction leaves room to ask another question before making a commitment.
