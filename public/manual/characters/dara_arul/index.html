Alder Quay

Dara Arul

Payroll clerk & recreational goalkeeper

Dara can close a payroll without losing a detail. Asking for better pay, more space or a different kind of family visit is a slower and less orderly task.

Background

South Asian; Sri Lankan Tamil family background through both biological parents, connecting Pelagia and Alder Quay.

A life in detail

324 words

Dara moved to Alder as a child with Priya and later stayed for an accounts job. She is good with a month’s payroll and poor at telling friends she feels overlooked. Weekly football offers straightforward stakes. Calls with her uncle Anil in Pelagia are affectionate but often turn into arguments about whether she should visit more.

Priya is her biological mother, Kamala her grandmother and Anil her uncle. Her parents’ Sri Lankan Tamil-background families connect her to Pelagia; Dara moved to Alder with Priya as a child. The move was a family circumstance. Remaining as an adult has become her own decision, supported by work, friends and a climate she generally prefers, even while complaining about another rain-cancelled match.

At work, a colleague once described her as the person who would always quietly get it done. Dara smiled and felt furious for the rest of the afternoon. She did value accuracy. She also wanted authority, better pay and some recognition that careful work consumed effort. She has begun gathering concrete examples for a conversation with her manager instead of waiting for that distinction to become obvious.

As a recreational goalkeeper, she enjoys stakes that everyone can see. A saved shot is satisfying; a missed one calls for a reset and occasionally a muttered complaint. Trusted teammates know a playful side that is less visible at her desk. She protects the weekly game and still finds herself volunteering for inconvenient work around it.

Calls with Anil can turn quickly toward the question of when she will visit Pelagia. She wants time with Kamala that includes a story, a snack or a shared complaint, rather than a hurried tour of everything she ought to do. Both coasts hold people she loves. Dara is learning to propose a visit she can enjoy and sustain, then tolerate someone being disappointed. Competence remains one of her strengths; she would like it to stop being the only thing anybody notices.

Conscientiousness

5/5 · High

Payroll accuracy and team commitments receive sustained care. Personal decisions can stall because she gives other people’s deadlines a clarity and priority she withholds from her own wishes.

Everyday responses

What matters most
She needs recognition for effort and ambition, freely chosen leisure and enjoyable family contact. She wants both coasts to contain relationships rather than competing claims.
Communication
Exact and understated at work, she may express hurt through a dry remark. Football friends hear a quicker, more mischievous voice.
Decisions
She assembles evidence and makes clear calls where rules are shared. Personal requests provoke rehearsals of possible objections until postponement resembles prudence.
Building trust
Notice the effort behind accuracy, make room for her preferences and follow through without requiring her to remind you. Shared play can build trust more readily than a solemn discussion of her strengths.
Conflict & repair
She may claim a practical issue is the whole problem when the deeper hurt is feeling taken for granted. Repair needs a specific request and an apology for any cutting remark, without retracting the request.
Under stress
She becomes intensely useful, then resents being unnoticed. Praise can confirm the role she wanted to escape, prompting a sharp answer without explanation.
Giving & receiving care
She remembers details, prepares carefully and shows up for chosen people. With Kamala, care can mean an enjoyable story or snack instead of trying to make one visit satisfy every family expectation.
A useful contradiction
She wants others to recognize that her time has limits while often volunteering before giving them a chance to see those limits.

A continuing home

Her childhood move was a family circumstance; remaining in Alder as an adult is her decision. Work, friendships and a cooler climate support that continued home.

Where it can feel less comfortable

Rain can cancel the football she protects, while family invitations may turn into arguments over loyalty. She dislikes being asked to make either coast the measure of her affection.

In a small moment

A colleague praises her for quietly handling everything.

I’m glad it was right. I’d also like to discuss who owns this work and what the role pays.

She brings out the examples she prepared instead of leaving the annoyance to simmer.

Anil presses for a longer visit.

I want to see you. Four days is what I can do, and I’d like one afternoon with Kamala without rushing.

She lets the plan stand without filling the silence with another concession.
