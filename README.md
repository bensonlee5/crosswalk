# Crosswalk

**[Play Crosswalk](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/)** · [Travel guide](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/manual/) · [Player guide](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/guide/)

Make a life, get to know your neighbors, and decide what matters to you. Crosswalk is a turn-based neighborhood life game for **1–4 players**, played solo, pass-and-play, or online in your browser.

## A little time, a lot of possibilities

Across eight representative months, balance work, home, friendships, learning, and the things you enjoy. Each month gives you six priority blocks to spend. Browse at your own pace: the clock moves when you confirm an action that costs time.

- Walk through illustrated isometric neighborhoods, with five regions painted in different styles.
- Meet regular neighbors and occasional visitors across generations, with family ties, their own hopes, and different amounts of time to give.
- Get to know people through short dialogue bubbles, visible gestures, shared experiences, and choices they remember.
- Notice everyday actions become familiar routines, with brief responses that change as you return.
- In shared rooms, supper orders and shared-room bookings can open or change opportunities for other players; local hosts keep these crossings moving in solo play too.
- Apply for work at named venues, build relevant experience, and make time for training. People you know can point you toward opportunities elsewhere.
- Encounter occasional small surprises during everyday activities, with the actual result shown when the action finishes.
- See your choices remembered in your journal and the life you build.

There is room to recover from setbacks. Your story is more than a points total.

## Start playing

1. [Open the game](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/) and create a room, or join a friend's room with its code.
2. Try the optional in-game tutorial, or open **Places** and choose somewhere to visit.
3. Open **Work & learning** to compare entry jobs and career paths. Visit the employer, apply, and separately accept an offer before choosing a shift.
4. Preview an activity's time, cost, and outcome before confirming. Review your bills before ending the month.

The [Crosswalk Travel Guide](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/manual/) starts with five town chapters, covers all 75 visitable venues and meaningful local objects, and includes all 68 character backstories, portraits, personality profiles, regional histories and linked families. The [player guide](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/guide/) explains how to play. The game has 39 regular neighbors and occasional visitors, plus seven additional lead counterparts in its crossing stories. All 68 people belong to the world; contextual mentions do not imply a scheduled map encounter. Twenty playable threads connect decisions, objects, work and other people’s plans.

## Find your way around

Click or tap a building, or use **Places** to choose a destination. Drag to pan the illustrated map and pinch or scroll to zoom. **Find me** centers your character; **Entire map** shows the neighborhood. With the map focused, arrow keys pan, **+ / −** zoom, and **L** opens Places. Sound and music controls are in the game menu. Recorded action sounds include a sip for coffee, turning pages, cooking and repairs. Sound needs a tap or key press to start; use **Test coffee sip** to check it, and adjust or mute effects and music separately.

## Work, learning and setbacks

Choose from café service, technical, practical and civic career paths, plus separately chosen short commitments. Entry jobs need no earlier job or contact. Campus courses cost time and money; longer Library preparation is free. A reference can help, but each employer still checks the role’s requirements.

Unpaid household bills become arrears. Repeated missed settlements lead to shared housing, an administration commitment and wage repayments. Free learning, entry applications, rest and relationships remain available while you recover. The game shows these consequences before settlement.

Every life uses the same monthly rules for careers, financial recovery and everyday surprises.

## Keep your game

Guest play is available now. Return in the **same browser and keep its site data** to retain access to your saved games. A room code alone cannot recover your guest seat. Email accounts and cross-device recovery are currently unavailable.

Saved lives from earlier game formats are unsupported. Start a new life to play the current game. Older stored records are not migrated or deleted.
