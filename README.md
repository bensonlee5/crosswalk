# Crosswalk

**[Play Crosswalk](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/)** · [Player guide](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/guide/)

Make a life, get to know your neighbors, and decide what matters to you. Crosswalk is a turn-based neighborhood life game for **1–4 players**, played solo, pass-and-play, or online in your browser.

## A little time, a lot of possibilities

Across eight representative months, balance work, home, friendships, learning, and the things you enjoy. Each month gives you six priority blocks to spend. Browse at your own pace: the clock moves when you confirm an action that costs time.

- Walk through illustrated isometric neighborhoods, with five regions painted in different styles.
- Meet regular neighbors and occasional visitors across generations, with family ties, their own hopes, and different amounts of time to give.
- Get to know people through conversations, shared experiences, and choices they remember.
- Build a career, make things, or put time into your community.
- See your choices remembered in your journal and the life you build.

There is room to recover from setbacks. Your story is more than a points total.

## Start playing

1. [Open the game](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/) and create a room, or join a friend's room with its code.
2. Try the optional in-game tutorial, or open **Places** and choose somewhere to visit.
3. Preview an activity's time, cost, and outcome before confirming. Review your bills before ending the month.

The [player guide](https://bensonlee5.github.io/crosswalk/guide/) includes illustrated local histories and 68 character backstories, organized by region, without future story spoilers. New games draw on 39 regular neighbors and occasional visitors; the wider cast also includes people whose lives remain in the background. Older saved rooms retain their original cast.

## Find your way around

Click or tap a building, or use **Places** to choose a destination. Drag to pan the illustrated map and pinch or scroll to zoom. **Find me** centers your character; **Entire map** shows the neighborhood. With the map focused, arrow keys pan, **+ / −** zoom, and **L** opens Places. Sound and music controls are in the game menu.

## Keep your game

Guest play is available now. Return in the **same browser and keep its site data** to retain access to your saved games. A room code alone cannot recover your guest seat. Email accounts and cross-device recovery are currently unavailable.

[Games started on the original site stay there](https://crosswalk-life-game.bensonlee5.chatgpt.site); they do not transfer automatically. Older saved rooms keep their original rules.
