# Crossing-story verification

## Production scope

Twenty threads use current authoritative commands, actual venue visits, money, priority blocks, paid-hour limits and household bills. Three longer anchors involve Whaleskin, Dalloway and Bracken. Each thread has three finite personal aftermath stages, with 169 conditional alternatives across the sixty stages. The Travel Guide supplies canonical people, venue names, objects, brands and ownership; conditional story outcomes do not rewrite biographies.

The world has 68 canonical people. The core encounter system uses 39 identities; ensemble lead scenes add seven, for 46 directly interactive identities. The 53 presentation profiles include contextual people and must not be treated as additional scheduled map actors.

## Defects caught before release

- Regional openings originally expired before normal travel unlocked. Setups now activate on an actual first visit. Real new-life routes verify month-six and month-eight Pelagia openings without added cash or teleportation. Solo players can spend four blocks across an anchor; each multiplayer seat is capped at two main beats per thread per month.
- Globally consumed scenes originally left later local turns empty. Finite, private personal aftermath now retains source-dependent stakes after the shared resolution, without respawning crises or duplicating owned work and stock.
- Removing private receipts alone left exact private alternatives in ordinary world tracking and departure history. Response projection now strips these other-seat histories too. Actor state remains complete. Actual HTTP and PostgreSQL tests cover reads, moves, stored replay, reload, travel and saved lists.

## Independent natural-life comparison

The same 84 eight-month policies ran before and after the aftermath change: 1–4 seats, seven policies, three seeds, 1,680 player-months per pass. They used real career application/acceptance, work, bills and paid travel. Action counts were 6,894 before and 8,060 afterward.

Among 36 engaged multiplayer lives, every-seat direct-other-player offers rose from 14 to 34. Excluding purchase-only continuing arrangements, the count rose from 3 to 29. All tested local, cause-first and late-interest multiplayer seats received direct non-purchase consequences. Later local turns no longer ran out of available story choices. Geographically separated travelers retained exceptions, including two lives where a seat received no direct-other-player offer. These fixed-policy measurements are not population probabilities or a guarantee of human enjoyment.

Targeted independent checks include real arrival traces, 137 original visible alternatives, 120 available aftermath alternatives on one prior-choice path, actual ReactDOM interactions, refunds, paid-work atomicity, ownership, stale-source rejection and complete private-response scans. This is not exhaustive Cartesian coverage of all conditional choices.

## Narrative and lifecycle checks

The sixty foreground scenes were rewritten around distinct personalities, concrete motives, embarrassment, resentment, pride and comic misunderstandings. All 169 choice labels, personal outcomes, replies and activities changed. A structural SHA-256 regression retains every gate, cost, source, claim, public summary/effect and speaker identity. The public summaries currently participate in effect identity and must not be casually edited; separate stable IDs from display prose before changing that contract.

Actual ReactDOM Strict Mode and suspended-transition tests cover committed state, stale action callbacks, owner/seat replacement, pending replies, journal context and renderer teardown. Repository lint is clean and is a Pages CI gate. The full mechanical baseline passed 649 tests; the final source must also pass the aggregate and exact-bundle backend gate described in the release procedure.

## Limits

Native and simulated checks do not establish deployed browser layout, mobile touch behavior, audible playback, arbitrary human crossing density or sustained comedy. Public browser verification belongs to the exact deployed build. The three anchors have more elaborate reversals than the smaller local threads; the complete cast is not simultaneously present on the map.
