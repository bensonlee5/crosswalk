# Lives before the player arrived

## Authored scope and discovery

All 26 identities retain their established arcs and gain a specific earlier life plus a deeper personal recollection. `lib/character-histories.ts` contains 28 selective connections spanning family, friendship, reciprocal help, working acquaintances and correspondence. Everybody has at least one connection; nobody knows everybody. New family facts include Oren and older brother Miro, and Leila and younger brother Tomas.

Each edge has one concrete shared factual event and two perspectives. Reversing an edge returns the same event. Perspectives differ in emphasis, never in the basic facts. Existing Mina/Eli/Jo dynamics remain old friendship, practical collaboration, credit and bounded care.

Open a focused encounter, then “Before your paths crossed.” Earlier-life accounts are available at first meeting. Deeper recollections and personal connections require actual familiarity from saved cast phases, local conversations, Neri’s stages or main-story receipts. The other person’s perspective requires knowing them too. Browsing and reloading earn nothing. There is no global collection directory. Redacted online seats get no profile projection.

These are fictional narrative-discovery gates in client content, not a claim that authored plot text is a server secret. Actual player choices and private cast memories remain server-sanitized by ownership.

## Later moments with consequences

Four optional conversations can reopen a completed newcomer friendship in month six or later, provided the player also actually knows the named other person:

- Bram/Tess: a generous lighted room must not become a promise of unlimited opening hours.
- Oren/Jo: credit a completed frame repair without volunteering its maker for a larger production.
- Sami/Tomas: an open game chair need not become an old friend’s permanent testing shift.
- Kit/June: knowing the cook does not supply permission to publish a recognizable difficult shift.

The NPC does not silently learn about the player’s acquaintance. The player explicitly mentions only that they know the other person, or declines to speak for them. No private conversation is quoted. The involved friend changes their own draft, invitation or plan; nobody else is signed up, forgiven or assigned feelings. The choice becomes a saved memory and affects the ending. These are personal continuations, not global NPC behavior changes in another player’s story.

A considered contribution costs one existing priority block; stating a boundary costs zero. Neither grants cash, energy, happiness or relationship points. Each event occurs at most once and respects the one-response-per-person-per-month rule. Existing six-block monthly pacing, promises, repair and hopeful unfinished exits remain.
